Privacy Policy — Presales Force

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This policy explains what the Presales Force mobile app (ai.presalesforce.seos) collects, why, who it is shared with, and what you can do about it.

In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Vaibhav Srivastava, an individual developer based in India who operates the Presales Force app, 538K/1707/11, Triveni Nagar-2, Sitapur Road, Lucknow 226020. You can reach us at vaibhav.srivastava@presalesforce.ai.

1. In short

  • We collect the account details you give us and the learning activity you generate by using the app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. There is no ad network, no attribution SDK, and no behavioural profiling.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • You can view and correct your details, and permanently delete your account and its data, from inside the app.

2.2 Information created by using the app

  • Learning progress — which framework modules you have started or completed, and your quiz scores.
  • Content views — which articles or lessons you have opened, and when.
  • Activity days — the calendar dates (in UTC) on which you used the app. This is what produces your Day Streak. We record the date only, not the time of day and not what you did.
  • Game results — scores from the practice game, used for your TWCI Score and XP.
  • Community memberships — which communities you have joined.
  • Notification preferences and history — the notifications we have sent you and which categories you have enabled.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No precise or approximate location. The "location" field in your profile is free text you type yourself, such as a city name.
  • No contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, or microphone data.
  • No background collection. The app requests only the INTERNET permission. Your photo library is opened by the Android system picker, and only when you choose to set an avatar or attach an image; we receive only the image you pick.

3. How we use it

We use your data to operate the service: to authenticate you, show your progress, compute your streak and scores, display community content, send the notifications you have enabled, and email you a password-reset code when you ask for one.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Depending on where you live, our legal bases are performance of a contract (operating your account), consent (optional profile fields, notifications), and legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and working).

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, we delete it. Your profile, learning progress, content views, activity days, game scores, community memberships, comments, reactions, messages and notifications are removed from our live database immediately. Curated feed posts remain, with any link to you removed.
  • Password-reset codes expire 10 minutes after they are issued and are deleted when used or when the account is deleted.
  • Encrypted backups may retain data for up to 30 days after deletion before they are rotated out.
  • Server logs are kept for 30 days for security and debugging.

6. Your rights and choices

Wherever you are, you can:

  • See and correct your data — Profile → Personal information.
  • Change your password — Profile → Change password.
  • Control notifications — Profile → Notifications.
  • Delete your account and its data — Profile → Delete Account. You will be asked for your password to confirm. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can also email us at vaibhav.srivastava@presalesforce.ai to request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your data in a portable format, restriction of processing, or to object to processing. We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or UK you may complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in India, you may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer, Vaibhav Srivastava, at vaibhav.srivastava@presalesforce.ai, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as cryptographic hashes and are never readable by us or recoverable in plain text. Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS. Access to production systems is restricted, and database backups are taken before schema changes.

Sessions use a signed token that expires after 30 days. When you delete your account, existing sessions stop working immediately.

No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant regulator where the law requires it.

8. Children

The app is intended for working professionals and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
